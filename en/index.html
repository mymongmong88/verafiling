Can you help with a renewal handled by another provider?

We first review your existing registration records, account access, submission authorization, and current contract. If a U.S. Agent appointment is bundled into that arrangement, its continuity must also be addressed. We then outline the work we can support and the fees; cancellation or transfer is not automatic.

How do registration and product listing differ?

Facility registration concerns the facility that manufactures or processes cosmetics. Product listing concerns marketed cosmetic products. We review the manufacturing facility and the Responsible Person—the manufacturer, packer, or distributor named on the label—to distinguish the relevant work.

Do small brands always need to register?

Certain small businesses may be exempt from facility registration and product listing. Eligibility depends on average U.S. cosmetic sales over the previous three years, inflation adjustments, and product-type exceptions. An exemption does not remove every other cosmetic regulatory obligation.

How long does it take, and when do I renew?

Timing depends on document readiness and prerequisites such as an FEI number. We outline preparation and submission timing in the quote, without guaranteeing FDA processing times. Facility registrations renew every two years from initial registration, while product listings require annual updates.

Is a U.S. Agent included for overseas facilities?

Foreign facilities subject to registration need a U.S. Agent. Filing assistance and U.S. Agent appointment are separate roles. We confirm whether you already have an agent and whether appointment assistance or any related fees are included in your quote.

Will I receive an FDA certificate?

FDA does not issue official certificates verifying MoCRA registration or listing compliance. Available deliverables are actual submission details and system-status records, not FDA approval or certification.

What should I include in my first inquiry?

Your company name, contact details, service needs, and approximate facility or product count are enough. Do not send full formulas, ingredient tables, account passwords, or other confidential materials in your first inquiry. We will explain what is needed once the scope is clear.
