Service scope

Services support data preparation and submission for facility registration, renewal, and product listing. The individual scope, fee, schedule, and cancellation or refund terms are confirmed in a written quotation and agreement before work begins.

Fees and timing

Published prices describe the indicated base scope. Additional products, document preparation, and amendments may need a separate quote. We do not guarantee FDA processing, approval, or market-entry outcomes.

Client information and authorization

Clients must review the accuracy of supplied information and provide the authority required for submissions. Filing assistance does not replace product safety substantiation, label compliance, or fulfillment of all legal obligations.

Independence and records

VeraFiling is a private service not affiliated with FDA. Registration and listing are not an approval or certification program. No official FDA registration certificate is provided.
